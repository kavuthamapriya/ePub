Preface

This book is the result of endless efforts and contributions of extremely renowned scholars from various inter-disciplinary backgrounds. The idea for this book was conceptualized while teaching a course on media studies and organizing a Seminar at the Department of Sociology at Jamia Millia Islamia, New Delhi, which finally took its shape at CSSS, JNU, New Delhi. My teaching of the course on social theory and supervision of PhD students in the media studies further galvanized me to develop a book on the issue of media and marginals.

I am immensely grateful to two institutions, the Jawaharlal Nehru University and Jamia Millia Islamia; and all my students and colleagues for their immense support. This book is dedicated to my students, and the teachers at the said universities.

The book is prepared for scholars in sociology, media studies, marginal studies, and social sciences, in general. It is a multidisciplinary work on media and marginal studies with the objective of exploring and understanding the issues related to marginals in Indian society, references to medium, message and its meaning. In continuation of the sociological and media studies tradition for understanding marginals, this volume moves beyond the conventional methodological practices.

The book is an outcome of the collective endeavour of all contributors, and enables scholars to understand and explore the question of media and marginals from the macro to the micro level. The introduction provides a background to this book while discussing the question of the marginal. All the chapters have emphasized on the different dimensions and approaches of the medium, message and meaning. It engages with various questions from sexuality to feminism; the Dalit to disability in cinema; minority to agitation and social movement; labour to theatre; and representation to ideological domination. It uncovers the ontological and epistemological questions related to media and marginals. In general, all the chapters in this book present an entwined analysis of the question related to media and marginals.

I am thankful to Prof Biswajit Das, Prof Neshat Quaiser, Dr Vibodh Parthasarathi, and all my colleagues, students, departmental staff and library staff members of the JNU for their constant support.

I would personally like to thank Mr. Ajay Kumar Jain and Mr. Ananya Jain of Manohar Publications and Distributors for publishing this volume. Many thanks to the anonymous readers of the manuscript for their detailed comments, and the entire Manohar team.

Many thanks to my family members for their unwavering support.
